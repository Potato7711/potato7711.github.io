Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

What Clipper is

Clipper is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It cuts short clips from long videos and posts them to the owner's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts. Nobody else can sign up or use it.

What it reads from the platforms

  • TikTok: the account's display name and picture, permission to send videos to the account, and the view, like, comment and share counts of the account's own videos.
  • YouTube: the channel name, permission to upload videos, and the channel's own video statistics (views, likes, comments, watch time).
  • Instagram: the account's username, permission to publish Reels, and the account's own post insights.

Where it is kept

Everything stays on the owner's computer. Clipper has no server of its own, does not sell or share any data, shows no ads and does not track anyone.

Removing access

Disconnect an account inside Clipper, and remove its access on the platform: TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions), Google (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or Instagram (Settings → Apps and websites). Clipper then deletes the saved sign-in for that account.

YouTube

Clipper uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles data under the Google Privacy Policy.
